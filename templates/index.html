Best Materials & Crack Tips

Top Coding Platforms

  • LeetCode - Essential for mastering Data Structures & Algorithms.
  • NeetCode.io - Highly structured roadmap for cracking coding interviews.
  • GeeksforGeeks - Excellent for core CS concepts and tutorials.
  • HackerRank - Great for beginner programming and database SQL practice.

Best YouTube Channels

  • freeCodeCamp.org - Full, multi-hour courses on literally everything.
  • takeUforward (Striver) - Best channel for DSA, specifically the A-Z SDE sheet.
  • Corey Schafer - Masterclass tutorials for Python.
  • ByteByteGo - Excellent animated system design explanations.

Quantitative & Aptitude

  • IndiaBix - The ultimate resource for Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, and puzzles.
  • Book: "Quantitative Aptitude" by R.S. Aggarwal.
  • Strategy: Master Percentages, Ratios, Time-Speed-Distance, and Work-Time concepts first.

How to Crack Interviews

  • Research: Know the company and role thoroughly before joining the call.
  • STAR Method: Answer behavioral questions using Situation, Task, Action, Result.
  • Mock Interviews: Practice with peers on Pramp or similar platforms.
  • Ask Questions: Always have 2-3 questions ready for the interviewer to show interest.
